Generates a predicted value of oxygen-18 enrichment in animal bodywater and
tooth enamel given all oxygen inputs (atmospheric oxygen, air water vapor,
bound oxygen in food, free water in food, and drinking water), oxygen outputs
(exhaled CO2, urea, fecal water, urine, respiratory water vapor,
transcutaneous water vapor, and sweat), and species-specific values (body
mass, water economy index) relative to environmental values (temperature,
oxygen-18 enrichment of surface water, and relative humidity).
